A make.fun entry for "The Battery's Last Text to Its Charger": one line of plain text (at most 200 characters) — the phone battery's final message to the charger it waited for all day.

> You had ONE job and you spent all day in the other room. I hope my last 1% haunts you.

`main.ts` writes that line to `/output/entry.txt`.
